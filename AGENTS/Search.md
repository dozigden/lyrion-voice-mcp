# Search Guidance

Read this before changing search contracts, ranking, observation capture, catalogue ingestion, or search storage.

- Production artist, album, and track search uses the catalogue-backed `catalogue-phuzzy-sqlite` resolver version 4. Version 4 carries the required native LMS rating, supports rating-constrained retrieval and rating browse, and stores canonical track-to-artist identities for exact-artist expansion. `ProductionCatalogueSearchService.Descriptor` is the authoritative production resolver identity; resolver and index-builder consumers must read the shared descriptor rather than repeat its name or version. Playlist discovery remains an isolated LMS `playlists` request for unconstrained searches.
- Keep resolver, candidate, execution, metric, and diagnostic contracts in Search production-neutral. The Evaluation executable consumes those contracts for benchmarking; deployed code must not depend on Evaluation types or the executable project.
- Return separate result groups with internal caps of 5 artists, 5 albums, 5 top tracks, 30 tracks, and 5 playlists. Top tracks must satisfy the effective search rating constraint and native rating 80 or above. The ordinary track pool still includes highly rated tracks, but prepare 35 ordinary candidates, remove selected top-track identities at the end, and then take 30 so the public groups do not overlap. Preserve LMS order within the playlist group. Keep the caps central and internal so they can be tuned without adding caller-selected limits or pagination.
- The public MCP search text field is `name`, not `query`, to signal that it accepts only artist, album, track, or playlist name text. Reject names with no normalised letter-or-digit token, over 500 characters, over 20 such tokens, or recognisable numeric rating syntax before starting either retrieval source. Correct misplaced rating syntax towards the separate `rating` and `ratingMatch` fields while continuing to allow ordinary numbered media names. Treat `*` as invalid rather than a wildcard and direct rating-only exploration to browse. Keep diagnostic and production limits aligned where they share validation.
- The production resolver uses bounded normalised, compact, acronym, consonant-skeleton, Double Metaphone, token/prefix, and trigram retrieval lanes, then applies the application-owned scorer. Preserve the distinction between retrieval evidence, ranking score, and confidence; no confidence or speculative no-match threshold is implemented.
- Track selection preserves descending relevance score bands, varies candidates only within an equal-score band, and spreads albums round-robin within that band. Top-track variation uses a soft positive rating weight within a relevance band: native 80 has weight 1, 90 weight 2, and 100 weight 3, with intermediate native ratings interpolated. Never sort top tracks strictly by rating or give an eligible four-star track zero probability. Use the same injectable selection algorithm for top and ordinary tracks so tests can remain deterministic while production searches rotate naturally.
- A unique exact whole-query artist match may expand tracks through the canonical artist identity stored in the published index. Do not expand when another artist has the same exact title or an album or track is also an exact whole-query match; fuzzy artist matches remain on ordinary search retrieval. Stream the artist's complete track relationship once into separate bounded 200-item ordinary and top-track reservoirs rather than accepting the retrieval lanes' artificial subset or materialising a large library. A future explicit artist-reference route may reuse this identity selector, but it is not implemented.
- Numeric tokens must contribute to phonetic evidence through spoken digit forms. Do not allow a phonetic encoder to silently discard a digit while treating the remaining words as a complete query span.
- Keep the public result contract independent of catalogue rows, SQLite documents, and LMS response shapes.
- The catalogue projection and disposable index retain required integer 0–100 track ratings internally. Only `lms-core` statistics feed this field; LMS missing and zero ratings both become native zero. Public MCP and observation mappings divide native values by 20 and expose decimal 0–5 numbers.
- An optional rating constraint always limits search to tracks and therefore skips playlist retrieval. `exact` matches only the corresponding native integer; a public decimal with no exact native representation matches nothing. `at_least` uses the smallest native integer at or above the public decimal. In particular, exact 0 selects native zero while at-least 0 selects every track. Apply the constraint inside every SQLite retrieval lane before its candidate limit, not after retrieval or ranking.
- Rating browse exposes floor buckets 0 through 5: native 0–19, 20–39, 40–59, 60–79, 80–99, and 100. Within a bucket order by native rating descending, then title, artist, album, and stable identity. Keep paging opaque at the MCP boundary.
- Search returns one opaque result handle per candidate; it does not return a separate public search identifier. Each handle combines candidate correlation with underlying LMS identity and is exposed as `browseRef`, `playRef`, or both according to capability.
- Returning the same media item from two searches must produce distinct result references for the two candidate occurrences.
- Artist, album, and playlist `browseRef` values can seed `browse`. Preserve correlation through derived browse descendants and continuations so successful playback or queue mutation marks the originating result selected. Artists and album artists are never directly playable.
- A production index is disposable derived data. Build only from storage-neutral `ICatalogueSearchDocumentSource` batches of 500 or fewer documents, including canonical main-track artist identities and album-artist fallback identities for tracks; never materialise the complete library in application memory.
- Build in a job-specific staging generation, validate it, move it into place, and atomically replace the small current-generation pointer. Continue serving the previous compatible generation throughout a rebuild.
- If no compatible published generation exists, return an explicit unavailable error. Do not build lazily and do not fall back to LMS artist, album, or track search.
- A successful catalogue refresh queues exactly one production index rebuild. Manual rebuild is a REST/UI administration action, not an MCP tool.
- The EF-backed operational observation store records original and trimmed query, optional rating constraint, resolver/version, retrieval sources, timings, ordered candidates with public ratings, zero-result searches, failures, later successful selections, and human reviews. Record unconstrained, requested-rating, top-rating, and exact-artist catalogue retrieval legs separately when they occur, including per-leg status, duration, and result count. Services owns its scopes, model/entity mapping, and retention; EF repositories own its queries.
- `SearchObservationRecorder` owns observation lifecycle metadata, DTO construction, source evidence, and best-effort persistence. Keep `SearchService` focused on validation, concurrent retrieval, candidate ordering, correlation, references, and the public outcome.
- If playlist retrieval fails, retain catalogue candidates and per-source failure evidence while failing the public search call. Treat failed searches separately from completed searches with no results.
- Observation recording is best effort and must not fail search, playback, or queue operations.
- Only mark candidates selected after their LMS mutation succeeds. Never mark skipped, failed, or unattempted batch items selected.
- Evaluation exports contain only explicitly included cases and omit observation IDs, LMS media IDs, correlation references, timestamps, and private notes. The canonical real corpus lives only in the private sibling repository.
- Spotify may be used only in a later offline experiment over recorded misses. It is not a runtime fallback or dependency.
- `GET /api/evaluation` and `POST /api/evaluation/search` expose diagnostics for the actual production resolver under the external name `production`. Keep lane and scoring evidence available without accepting or persisting corpus cases.
- Historical comparator evidence and the SQLite selection rationale live in `SEARCH_RESEARCH.md`; retired comparator implementations do not belong in production or Evaluation.
